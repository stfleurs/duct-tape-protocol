# Duct Tape Protocol — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 7 November 2026**

Duct Tape Protocol is a single-player mobile game developed by the Duct Tape Protocol project
("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game handles and how advertising works.
It applies to the Android and Web versions of the game.

**Short version:** the game has no account, no sign-in and no server of its own. Your progress is
stored only on your device. The game shows ads through Google AdMob, and AdMob may use your
device's advertising ID and related information to provide and measure those ads. In the EEA, UK
and Switzerland you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice at any time from
**SETTINGS → PRIVACY OPTIONS**.

---

## 1. Information the game itself stores

The game does not ask for your name, email address, phone number or contacts, and it does not
create an account or upload your save file.

The following is stored **locally on your device** and is not sent to us:

- game progress: scrap balance, unlocked levels, stars, badges, daily-shift and login-streak state;
- settings: sound, music and vibration preferences;
- ad frequency state: the number of finished shifts and the time of the last interstitial ad, so
  that ads are not shown too often.

You can delete all of this by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or by uninstalling
the game. The Web version stores similar data in your browser's local storage; clearing site data
removes it.

## 2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game uses the **Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob)** to show:

- **rewarded ads** — you choose to watch one to double the scrap earned after a shift;
- **interstitial ads** — full-screen ads only at natural breaks between shifts, at most every
  three finished shifts and never during gameplay;
- **banner ads** — currently disabled.

Google and its advertising partners may collect and process information such as:

- your device's **advertising ID** (Android Advertising ID) and other device or app identifiers;
- **IP address**, and an **approximate location** derived from it (usually city/region level);
- information about the **ad request and ad interactions** (for example, whether an ad was shown,
  viewed or clicked);
- **device and app information** (device model, operating system version, app version, language,
  network type);
- **diagnostic and performance information** used to keep ads working and to detect fraud.

This information is used to serve ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure ad
performance, prevent invalid traffic and fraud, and comply with legal obligations. It may be
shared with Google's advertising partners under their own privacy policies.

Google's handling of this data is described in:

- Google Privacy Policy: <https://policies.google.com/privacy>
- How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services:
  <https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>
- Google Advertising Policies: <https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads>

The game does not receive your advertising ID or any of the above information; it only asks the
SDK to show an ad.

## 3. Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

Before ads are requested, the game uses Google's **User Messaging Platform (UMP)** to ask for your
consent where required. If you do not consent, ads that require consent are not requested.

You can review or change your choice at any time:

1. open **SETTINGS** on the main menu;
2. tap **PRIVACY OPTIONS** (shown when Google says an entry point is required);
3. change your consent choices and save.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings
(**Settings → Google → Ads**, or **Settings → Privacy → Ads** on newer versions).

Where consent is not required, ads may rely on other legal bases permitted by applicable law. For
US state privacy laws, the same **PRIVACY OPTIONS** entry point lets you exercise applicable
opt-out rights when Google indicates it is required.

## 4. Sharing and sale of personal information

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it with anyone ourselves. Advertising
data is collected and processed by Google and its partners as described above. Depending on how
"sharing" or "sale" is defined in your jurisdiction (for example under the CCPA/CPRA), the
advertising ID processing described in Section 2 may be considered "sharing" for cross-context
behavioural advertising. You can opt out through **PRIVACY OPTIONS** where available, or by
resetting/limiting your advertising ID.

## 5. Retention

We do not retain personal information on any server. Locally stored game data remains on your
device until you clear it or uninstall the game. Google retains advertising data according to its
own policies and the choices you make with Google; see the Google Privacy Policy linked above.

## 6. Children

Duct Tape Protocol is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your
country) and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The game is designed
for teens and adults. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the
advertising SDK, contact us and we will help you exercise the available controls.

## 7. Security

The game itself does not transmit your save data. Ad requests are made over HTTPS by the Google
Mobile Ads SDK. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we keep the game's
own data handling to the minimum described here.

## 8. Changes to this policy

If the game's data practices change (for example, if a new SDK or online feature is added), this
policy will be updated and the date at the top will change. Material changes will be noted in the
release notes.
